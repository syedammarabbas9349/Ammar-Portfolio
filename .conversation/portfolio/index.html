Process

Every conversation runs the same five-step pipeline

The actual sequence a message goes through, from landing to resolution.

Intake

A message lands on any connected channel and gets normalized into one format.

2

Understand

The agent reads your product data or docs to work out what's being asked.

3

Respond

It replies in your business's voice — answering or moving the order forward.

4

Escalate

A serious lead gets handed to the least-busy human agent, with a summary.

5

Resolve

Once the human closes it, the agent resumes automatically — nothing sits idle.
